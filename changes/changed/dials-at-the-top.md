- The gauges at the top of the panel are now three dials, like a car's temperature gauge, with a word under each: Chip load (Light, Medium, or Heavy, over about the last 30 seconds), Memory (OK, Tight, or Full), and the temperature of whichever part is nearest its limit, labeled Chip temp, Battery temp, or Drive temp. Memory no longer shows a percentage that could read "51%" while memory was tight, and the labels are larger, with no headings over them.
