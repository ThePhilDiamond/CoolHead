- **Tools along the bottom, settings in the right-click menu.** The bottom of the panel is now a row of tools, each an icon with its name: Network (Test my network), Trends (the past week from macOS's power log, with a chart icon), Trusted, Report and Quit, each opening in the panel's middle. The Activity Monitor button is gone. The settings move into a **Settings** submenu of the right-click menu, each one a toggle: Notifications and Open at Login, then how Cool Head looks: a new Hide Tool Names, and Melting Snowflake Icon, which replaces the Menu Bar Icon choice. Check for Updates stays at the top of the menu, and About and Quit at the bottom.
  - **Battery**, a tool along the bottom, opens the battery's page: how much charge it holds against what Apple rates it for, what it's doing now (on battery, charging, or plugged in and why it isn't charging), Low Power Mode, and the switch for **Battery Saver**, the setting first called Save Battery When Unplugged, which you may turn on and off as your day changes. The battery line above the tools is gone. A Mac with no battery has no Battery tool.
  - Trusted is now a list in the panel, the standard macOS way: select one and press − to stop trusting it, or press + to choose an app to trust.
