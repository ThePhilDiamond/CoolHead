# The Cool Head name and logo

"Cool Head" and the Cool Head logo identify the official project and the builds published on this repository's Releases page. The MIT License covers the code, not the name or logo.

You are welcome to fork Cool Head. If you publish a fork or your own build:

- give it a different name and icon, so nobody mistakes it for the official app;
- you may say it is "based on Cool Head" and link back here;
- don't present it as an official Cool Head release, or imply the project endorses it.

Writing about Cool Head, linking to it, and using the name to refer to the official project is fine and doesn't need permission.
